## 2. #1 — the FOC complaint (the important one)

> **ATP Jean, 16:31:** 我们做了计算，发现 FOC 的算法错了。拿不到答案
> *("We did the calculation and found the FOC algorithm is wrong. We can't arrive at the answer.")*

Reference: [`01-meter-grid-foc-complaint.png`](Feedback/2026-08-07/01-meter-grid-foc-complaint.png),
[`07-Q1.1-foc-video-frame.png`](Feedback/2026-08-07/07-Q1.1-foc-video-frame.png)

### What they expect (their own formula, from Appendix A)

Their *Summary sales invoice meter listing* spells the arithmetic out, and it reconciles perfectly
on their sheet:

```
NET    = (Current TOTAL) − (Previous TOTAL) − FOC − Fixed Rebate QTY
CHARGE = NET × Rate
```

Verified against their own numbers — e.g. `61,432 − 59,565 = 1,867 × 0.03 = 56.01` ✓,
and the grand total `125.61 + 95.20 + 808.00 = 1,028.81` ✓.

### What the demo grid showed

I checked every row in the screenshot. **Every row with FOC Qty = 0 reconciles exactly.**
Only the two rows with a non-zero FOC Qty break — and they break in *opposite directions*:

| Machine | Meter | Usage | FOC Qty **shown** | Rate | Expected charge | **Actual charge** | Implied FOC |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CSSI 00002312 | 201-BK | 10,236 | **100** | 0.0300 | 304.08 | **292.08** | **500** |
| CSSI 00001664 | 223-COLOR | 6,368 | **200** | 0.3880 | 2,393.18 | **2,470.78** | **0** |

One deducted five times too much, the other deducted nothing. That is why they "can't get the
answer" — the number in the FOC Qty column is not the number the charge was calculated from.

### What I found in the book

I traced both machines in `AED_ATPTEST` (`zSCP2_ItemMeter`):

| Machine | Meter | **Stored FOCQty** | Multi-price | FOC reset |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CSSI 00002312 | 201-BK | **500** | *(none)* | M / 0 → ×1 |
| CSSI 00001664 | 223-COLOR | **0** | *(none)* | M / 0 → ×1 |

Run the engine's formula (`billed = usage − FOC × resetCount`) on the **stored** values:

- `(10,236 − 500) × 0.03` = **292.08** ← exactly what the invoice charged
- `(6,368 − 0) × 0.388` = **2,470.78** ← exactly what the invoice charged

> **So the charge engine is not miscalculating. It is arithmetically consistent with the data.**
> What is wrong is that the **FOC Qty column displayed 100 and 200** while the engine used
> **500 and 0**. The customer trusted the column, did the maths, and of course it didn't tie out.

**Caveat, stated plainly:** this book has been used for testing every day since the demo, so I
cannot prove the stored FOC was the same on 28/07 as it is today. Two possibilities remain:

1. The **column is reading FOC from a different source than the engine** — a real display bug.
2. The FOC data was **edited after the demo**, and on the day the engine genuinely used 100/200 —
   which would make the charges wrong.

Only #1 is consistent with the observed numbers (with FOC 100 and monthly reset the charge would
have been 304.08, not the 292.08 shown), so a display/source mismatch is by far the likelier story.
**Next step: reproduce on the demo book with the FOC Qty column and the invoice side by side.**

## 3. #10 — the credit note case study

Reference: [`03-Q10-credit-note-case-study.png`](Feedback/2026-08-07/03-Q10-credit-note-case-study.png)

Same billing history in both cases: Jul `0→1000`, Aug `1000→3000`, Sep `3000→4500`.
In October the meter turns out to read **less** than the 4,500 already billed.

**Q1 — actual meter is 2,800** (two CNs needed, because it falls below the Aug invoice's 3,000):

| Doc | Date | CN target reading | CN QTY |
|---|---|---|---|
| CN001 | Oct-26 | 3,000 | 1,500 (= 4,500 − 3,000, unwinds all of Sep) |
| CN002 | Oct-26 | 2,800 | 200 (= 3,000 − 2,800, bites into Aug) |
| Inv2610 | Oct-26 | previous meter = **2,800** | |

**Q2 — actual meter is 3,200** (one CN, because 3,200 is still above Aug's 3,000):

| Doc | Date | CN target reading | CN QTY |
|---|---|---|---|
| CN001 | Oct-26 | 3,200 | 1,300 (= 4,500 − 3,200) |
| Inv2610 | Oct-26 | previous meter = **3,200** | |

**This confirms our design, and it is exactly the case we fixed on 06/08.** Q1's CN002 corrects an
*earlier* invoice after a CN has already corrected a *later* one — the base for CN002 must be the
3,000 that CN001 left behind, not the Aug invoice's own billed figure. That is the cross-invoice
chain fix (commit `3f77b0c`): the CN base is now the newest correction on any invoice of that meter,
and the override row dates late enough that the newest CN genuinely wins.

**What still needs checking against Q1:** CN002 credits 200 copies, and those copies belong to the
**August** invoice, so the CN must be raised against Inv2608 while its base reads 3,000. Worth
walking the full Q1 sequence end-to-end on the demo book before showing it back to them.
